How I work

From research to results.

The same four steps for every brand, whether it’s SEO, ads or social.

01

Research

Audit the website, competitors and audience. Keyword research and an analytics baseline in GA4 and Search Console.

02

Strategy

Pick the channels, plan the content calendar, split the ad budget and agree the KPIs to track.

03

Launch

Publish content, run Meta and Google Ads, and ship on-page and technical SEO fixes.

04

Report & optimise

Track reach, CTR, CPC and conversions, send a monthly report, and keep improving what works.
